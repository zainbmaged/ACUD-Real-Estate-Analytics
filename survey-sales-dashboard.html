Where is dissatisfaction concentrated?

Share dissatisfied by district and service. Darker cells = higher share. Marker = evidence grade (hover for n, interval, comparison with other districts).

Markers: ● strong descriptive · ◐ consistent but limited · ○ preliminary · ⚠ survey and independent indicator disagree · blank = no concern detected or too few answers. Differences between districts are tested with Fisher's exact test, adjusted across all district × service comparisons (Benjamini–Hochberg).
