Aligned project-level scenario: both sections use total investment = planned units × assumed cost per unit. Projected cumulative sales = historical cumulative contract value + forecast contract value for the selected horizon. Project-level ROI proxy = (projected cumulative sales − total investment) / total investment × 100; recovery = projected cumulative sales / total investment × 100. This is not accounting profit or audited ROI. Investment inputs are synthetic; replace them with verified costs before real decisions. Projected break-even is an extrapolation, not a guarantee.
